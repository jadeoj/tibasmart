# TibaSmart visual direction

## Direction shortlist

### 1. Clinical Signal
A confident healthcare operating system with a dark control-room base, clear medical blue, and high-signal mint accents. Probability: 0.07.

### 2. Care in Motion
A bright, human-centered editorial system that uses soft surfaces, warm photography, and gentle motion to make complex healthcare workflows feel approachable. Probability: 0.06.

### 3. Orbit of Care
A modern healthcare infrastructure brand built around connected systems: deep navy space, floating cyan signal lines, crisp white panels, and a signature 3D orbit that represents every department moving as one. Probability: 0.08.

## Committed direction: Orbit of Care

- **Design movement:** Systems clarity with a sense of movement; the interface should make coordination feel visible, calm, and inevitable.
- **Core principles:** One operating layer, reduce cognitive load, show the system at work, earn trust through precision.
- **Color philosophy:** Deep navy is the operating environment; medical blue carries action and credibility; mint/cyan signals connected care and progress; white surfaces create breathing room.
- **Layout paradigm:** Wide editorial sections with a strong left-to-right reading rhythm, intentional asymmetry, rounded panels, and a single high-energy focal moment in the hero/trust area.
- **Signature elements:** A subtle grid, orbital arcs, soft radial glows, stacked workflow cards, small mono labels, and a 3D circular customer/partner orbit.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Motion is informative, not decorative. Hover and focus reveal depth or pause motion; every interactive surface has a visible focus state.
- **Animation:** Slow orbiting, small entrance reveals, gentle marquee movement, and CSS transitions. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with a still orbit and no auto-translation.
- **Typography system:** Plus Jakarta Sans for confident, contemporary headings and Inter/system fallback for body copy. Compact uppercase labels are used sparingly for wayfinding.
- **Brand essence:** Reliable technology that gives care teams more time for care.
- **Brand voice:** Direct, human, operationally fluent, and reassuring without sounding clinical or bureaucratic.
- **Wordmark/logo:** Reuse the existing TibaSmart wordmark with a clean white header lockup and a navy favicon treatment.
- **Signature brand color:** Medical blue `#1D8FF0`, supported by mint `#B7F8DC` and deep navy `#071A32`.
